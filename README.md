# RIVIO — Website

Official marketing website for **RIVIO** (rivioapp.com).

---

## What is RIVIO?

RIVIO is a pay-per-day fitness access platform. Users can use one app to access gyms, yoga studios, and wellness centers—paying only for the days they go, with no subscription or long-term commitment. Partners (gyms, studios, clubs) offer pay-per-day access and grow revenue through the RIVIO network.

**Tagline:** Your route to movement.

---

## What this repo is

- **rivio-web**: The public-facing website for rivioapp.com (landing, features, partner program, about, legal pages).
- **Tech:** Next.js (static export), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion.
- **SEO:** Sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`), `robots.txt`, and meta tags are generated for the production domain. See `SEO-GUIDE.md` for ongoing SEO steps.

---

## What we’re doing

- Marketing and explaining RIVIO’s pay-per-day model for users and partners.
- Driving app downloads and partner sign-ups.
- Hosting legal and support pages (terms, privacy, help, contact).
